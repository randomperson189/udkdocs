FaceFX Facial Animation

FaceFX is a facial animation solution developed by OC3 Entertainment and integrated with Unreal Engine 3.0. All Unreal Engine 3.0 licensees and UDK users have a license to FaceFX. This tool allows you to create complex animation sequences, specifically facial animations, that are synched to audio. These can be used for detailed cutscenes and cinematics or simply sequences played during the game to provide dramatically increased realism during dialog and other speech.
