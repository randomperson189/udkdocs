Lighting Artist

The mood of environments as well as theme and focal points are all impacted greatly by how the scene is lit. Lighting artists make use of the lighting tools to create both static and dynamic lighting that reflects these aspects. Having a comprehensive knowledge of all that Unreal Engine 3 offers as far as types of lights, types of shadows, and how they all work in conjunction will make lighting worlds in a manner that brings about the desired look and feel while staying within the limitations of the platforms being developed for much easier.
