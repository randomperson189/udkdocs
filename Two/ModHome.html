Mod Developer Home

Welcome to the Mod Developer section, with all the details you need to become an expert with the award-winning Unreal Technology.
If you are new to the site, please visit the Welcome page.
Once you are familiar with the site, stop by the Getting Started page to get started.

Here is where you'll find all the deep, detailed, interesting information on working with the engine in a technical fashion: from building your own game or mod, to networking or rendering specifications, as well as documentation on the practical, audio, visual and physical aspects of producing Unreal Engine 2 based mod content. Everything from level design to textures, music to modeling, will be covered here, in both practical, hands-on, how-to format, as well as in a more formal, comprehensive, reference format.
